# Using AnyOS in a business

AnyOS is free for personal use, hobby projects, study, research, and use by
charities, schools, public research bodies and government institutions, under
the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md).

Any other use, including use by or for a company, in a commercial product or
service, or for paid work, needs a separate commercial license from the
copyright holder. To ask for one, open an issue on the AnyOS GitHub repository
titled "Commercial license request" with your organisation's name and how you
plan to use AnyOS, and the maintainer will contact you. Please don't post
confidential details in the issue.

## Credit

Whatever license you use AnyOS under, keep the `Required Notice:` line and the
license terms with every copy, and don't present AnyOS, or work based on it, as
your own creation.
